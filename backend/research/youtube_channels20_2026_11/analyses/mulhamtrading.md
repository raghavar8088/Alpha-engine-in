"My Simple 1 Minute Scalping Strategy (Sniper Entry)" (2025-10-05, 18:47), https://youtu.be/Y1r7fTJ0FZ8
- Rules:
  - Mark 15-minute highs and lows that price has not reached yet [E 01:51-03:00].
  - A 15-minute candle takes one, closes back inside, and is coloured in the sweep's direction (a bullish candle taking a
    high) [E 03:00-05:31].
  - During the next 15-minute candle, enter when a 1-minute candle closes beyond the sweep candle's close (the "flip")
    [E 08:30-10:30].
  - Stop below the 1-minute breakout candle or swing, or (safer) beyond the 15-minute candle [E 10:00].
  - Target: the next 15-minute swing point [E 10:30-11:30].
  - Higher-probability levels: session highs and lows (Asia 20-24, London 02-05, New York 07-10 New York time), levels
    inside imbalances, structural levels [E 15:53-18:31].
  - Any market and any session [E 00:08].
- Code [A]:
  - XAUUSD and BTC.
  - 15-minute swings are fractals with n=2 that are still untouched. Session levels are not filtered.
  - Stop beyond the 15-minute sweep candle +/- 0.05 ATR (the safer option).
  - Target the nearest untouched 15-minute swing in the trade's direction. Skip when it is under 1R.
  - Flat at the clock.
- Relation: CP-26 turtle soup enters at the sweep close, with no 1-minute trigger and no swing target. MH-02 is kept as a

  - Entry: the first 1-minute FVG in the reversal direction within 30 minutes; limit at its midpoint.
  - Stop = sweep extreme +/- 0.1 x ATR(15m). Target = the other side of the Asian range (at least 1.5R).
