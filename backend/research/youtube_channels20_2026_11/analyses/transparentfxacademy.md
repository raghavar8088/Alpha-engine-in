## TF-01 Fibonacci confluence continuation (trend + flipped level + Fibonacci limit, stop past the next level, -0.272 target): ujcOxEsqSyo
"Simple Forex Trading Strategy: How to Catch 100 Pips Per Day ?" (2022-06-13, 22:33), https://youtu.be/ujcOxEsqSyo
- Trend: the last swing in the opposite direction has been broken (an uptrend = a break above the last high) [E 08:35-10:00].
- Structure: the broken level, i.e. previous resistance turned support (or support turned resistance) [E 02:00-05:00, 10:00].
- Fibonacci on the impulse leg. The confluence is the first Fibonacci level just beyond the structure on the pullback side
  (bearish example: 0.5 just below the flipped level; bullish example: 0.382 just above it). A limit order goes there
  [E 10:00-15:00].
- Stop beyond the next Fibonacci level, "more than 50% of the area" toward the level after it (a 0.5 entry puts the stop
  past 0.618 toward 0.786; a 0.382 entry puts it "closer to the 0.618") [E 10:00-11:30, 14:30].
- Target: the -0.272 extension of the impulse, aiming for about 1:3 [E 11:00, 15:00]. Risk 1% [E 17:11-20:00].
- Code [A]:
  - XAUUSD and BTC 15-minute (the video shows forex pairs).
  - Swings are fractals with n=3. A break is a close beyond the last confirmed swing high (or low).
  - Impulse: from the swing low before the break to the highest high since.
  - Entry levels 0.382 / 0.5 / 0.618. Stop = next level + 0.6 x (the level after it - the next level).
  - The limit stays valid for 24 bars, or until a new impulse extreme.
- Status: requires documented assumptions (how swings are chosen).
