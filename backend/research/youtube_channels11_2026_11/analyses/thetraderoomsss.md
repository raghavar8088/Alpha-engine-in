## TR-01 EMA 9/15 scalp on three entry candles, 1:2 (RMwUsnJB40c, 11 Jun 2023, 19 min, 5.4M views)

Market: BANKNIFTY options (buy a strike about 100 points in the money, E 00:04:30), 5-minute candles for traders
with a few months' experience, lower timeframes later (E 00:08:30). Read the rule parts in full (digest).

- Indicators: EMA 9 and EMA 15; trade the side they point to (up for buys, E 00:07:30).
- Entry candles for buys (E 00:07:00-00:11:00), at the moving averages: (1) a pin bar (long lower wick) touching the
  EMAs; (2) a big-bodied bullish candle with a small lower wick (often a bullish engulfing); (3) a candle that crosses
  the EMA from below and closes above it. Buy above the entry candle's high; stop at its low; target 1:2 (hit within
  about one candle in his examples). Sells mirror it with the three opposite candles.
- Small stop, quick exits: scalping keeps the time in the trade short (E 00:00:30-00:01:00, 00:06:30).
- The shorts and live episodes (u7Npiub4iAA, xMuD2ueoN-8, slUuTYS7RUQ: bitcoin on 15 minutes with the same EMA
  rejections; XurmC12Wp08, CeHFDebhZwA: NIFTY/BANKNIFTY live scalps adding size only when the trade moves his way,
  exiting on a close below a trendline or round number) show the same method traded live; they add no fixed rules.
- A: BANKNIFTY 5-minute; trend up = EMA 9 above EMA 15 and both higher than 3 candles ago; "touching" = the candle's
  low at or below EMA 9 while its close stays above EMA 15; pin bar = lower wick at least 2 x the body; big body = body
  at least 60% of the range and at least the 20-candle average body; cross candle = open below EMA 9, close above it;
  a stop-entry at the high valid for the next 2 candles; stop at the candle's low; target 2R; up to 4 trades a day,
  stopping after 2 stops; out at the session's end.
