## TH-01 Bitcoin 6 PM session: anchored VWAP + EMA 25, enter on the 5-minute "N", exit on the 15-minute reverse "N"
(57a1dixbtb4, 2 Sep 2026, 20 min; ukIfXqaQ7hs, 19 Aug 2026, the same rules without the session)

Read the rule parts of both in full.

- Session (57a1dixbtb4 description; E 00:01:00-00:01:30): five years of data show the biggest bitcoin moves between
  18:00 and 23:00 IST; trade only then; at most two trades a day.
- Lines (E 00:01:30-00:02:30): VWAP anchored at bitcoin's day open (06:00 IST) and EMA 25.
- Entry (E 00:03:00-00:04:30): price closes above both lines; do not chase the straight run; wait for an "N" on 5
  minutes: a pullback and then a close above the previous high (shorts mirror it below both lines).
- Exit (E 00:04:30-00:05:30, 00:08:00): after entry ignore the two lines; manage on 15 minutes; exit only when the
  15-minute chart forms the reverse "N" (a lower high and a break of the previous low for a long).
- His 5-minute stop (ukIfXqaQ7hs E 00:06:30): a close below the EMA with price action confirming. Leverage at most 5x,
  at most 20% of capital (57a1dixbtb4 E 00:09:30).
- A: BTCUSD; entries 18:00-23:00 IST; VWAP from 06:00 IST (00:30 UTC) on 5-minute bars; EMA 25 of 5-minute closes; a
  long = a confirmed 3-candle fractal high, then a confirmed fractal low after it (the pullback), then a 5-minute close
  above that fractal high while above both lines; enter at the next open; hard stop 0.1 x ATR 14 below the pullback
  low; exit at the next open after a 15-minute close below the latest confirmed 15-minute fractal low (3-candle);
  at most 2 trades a day; one position at a time; shorts mirror it.

## TH-03 Renko + Ichimoku dual system, gold (Vjt4ZKD8pbI, 7 Aug 2026, 27 min)

Read in full, with the description's rule list.

- Renko (E 00:01:00-00:09:00): bricks form only on a fixed box move, so time and noise drop out; box size (description):
  crypto about 1% (ether 15), gold 43 (traditional).
- Ichimoku on the Renko chart (E 00:10:00-00:11:00): only the base line and the cloud.
- Rules (description): long entry = a brick closes above the base line and the cloud; short entry = a brick closes below
  both; long exit = a brick closes inside the cloud (the cloud trails, the base line is not used); short exit = a brick
  closes above the base line (crypto falls fast, so shorts use the faster line; E 00:12:00-00:13:00).
- A: XAUUSD; traditional Renko from 1-minute closes with a 43-dollar box; Ichimoku 9 / 26 / 52 computed on the brick
  series (the cloud displaced 26 bricks); signals act at the next 1-minute open after the brick completes; one position
  at a time; no other stop.

## TH-05 EMA 50 / 200 trend, pullback to EMA 50 with RSI 40-55, bitcoin 15 minutes (Oxy1x7_BPwQ, 7 Jan 2026, 33 min)

- Entry (E 00:06:45-00:08:00): price comes back to EMA 50; a green candle closes there with RSI 14 between 40 and 55;
  enter at its close.
- Target (E 00:08:15-00:10:30): a 15-minute close with RSI in the 70-75 zone books the trade. Stop (E 00:10:30): the

- A: BTCUSD 15-minute; EMA 50 above EMA 200 and both higher than 4 candles ago; a candle whose low reaches EMA 50 (within

  lowest low of the last 10 candles; exit at the next open after a close with RSI at or above 70, or after a close with
  RSI below 40; shorts mirror it (RSI 45-60, target RSI at or below 30, exit above 60).

## TH-08 EMA 288 (24 hours) trend, Supertrend signal, enter on the base break, trail behind swings, 5 minutes
(1yfmy2C5Yz0, 19 Dec 2024, 26 min)

Read the rule parts in full.

- Trend (E 00:05:30-00:07:00): 5-minute candles; EMA 288 = the last 24 hours; above it bullish, below it bearish.
- Entry (E 00:10:30-00:11:00): a Supertrend signal in the trend's direction is not enough; wait until price breaks the
  base it built (the swing low for a short) and enter on that candle; stop at the swing high.
- Exit (E 00:11:30): trail the stop to each new swing; exit when the latest swing high breaks (a "W"); ignore the
  Supertrend's colour after entry.
- A: BTCUSD 5-minute; Supertrend (10, 3) (settings not stated); a short when the close is below EMA 288, the Supertrend
  is down, and a candle closes below the latest confirmed 3-candle fractal low formed since the Supertrend turned down;
  enter at the next open; stop 0.1 x ATR above the latest confirmed fractal high; the stop moves down to each newer
  confirmed fractal high; longs mirror it; one position at a time.

## TH-09 Darvas box at 52-week highs (6j9aagTNFJc, 31 Oct 2023, 16 min)

Read the rule parts in full.

- E 00:01:30-00:04:00: buy only strength: a stock at a 52-week high that pauses and forms a box (consolidation); buy
  the breakout of the box top; stop below the box; as new boxes form higher, trail the stop to each new box bottom;
  unlimited upside, risk only the box.
- A: the NSE daily pool; Darvas's box: after a 52-week closing high, the box top = a high not exceeded for the next 3
  sessions, the box bottom = the lowest low after the top that is not broken for 3 sessions; entry = a close above the
  box top, at the next open; stop 0.1 x ATR 14 below the box bottom; each new completed box above the entry moves the
  stop to its bottom; time stop 60 sessions; long only.
