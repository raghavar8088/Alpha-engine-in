## SY-01 Higher-timeframe structure, 1-hour trend break entered on 15 minutes (GuhUEZglNYE, 6 May 2023, 13 min)

Market: bitcoin (any market). Read in full. The title's "94% win rate" is not tested in the video.

- Structure (E 00:00:30-00:07:00): uptrend = higher highs and higher lows, downtrend = lower highs and lower lows;
  read weekly, daily and 4-hour structure to know the overall direction and realistic targets; no indicators.
- Entry (E 00:08:00-00:10:00): on 1 hour wait for the current trend to break; a break to the downside = short, to the
  upside = long; set an alert at the level, then drop to 15 minutes and enter when the break happens there.
- Stop and target (E 00:10:00): stop above the previous high (short), target beyond the previous low at about 1:2
  (1:2-1:3).
- Management (E 00:12:00): at about 50% of the way to the target move the stop to breakeven and start taking profit.
- A: BTCUSD; a 1-hour uptrend = the last two confirmed 5-candle fractal highs and lows rising; the break = a 15-minute
  close below the last 1-hour higher low (mirror: a close above the last lower high of a 1-hour downtrend); enter at
  the next open; stop 0.1 x 15-minute ATR above the latest confirmed 3-candle fractal high on 15 minutes; target 2R;
  stop to breakeven at +1R (half-way); one trade per break.
