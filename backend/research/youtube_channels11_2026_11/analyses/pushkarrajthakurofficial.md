## PR-01 Brahmastra: Supertrend (20, 2) + MACD + VWAP, 5 minutes (0EXVG4kPAm0, 28 Dec 2022, 31 min, 2.3M views;
ctpGuhCCAtE, 31 Oct 2022; U3_47LWge-Y, 13 Jul 2022)

Read the rule parts of 0EXVG4kPAm0 in full and the other two in digest.

- Indicators (E 00:02:00-00:04:00, 00:11:00-00:12:00): VWAP; Supertrend made faster, length 20 and factor 2 instead of
  the default 10 and 3; MACD with its default settings. He opens BankNifty futures because the spot index has no
  volume for VWAP (E 00:01:00-00:02:30).
- Timeframe (E 00:15:00): 5 minutes for intraday trades (15 minutes is slower).
- Entry (E 00:13:30, 00:15:00-00:16:00, 00:23:00): a Supertrend buy signal and a MACD bullish cross (the MACD line
  crossing above the signal line); the two may come up to three or four candles apart; then wait for the next candle
  to break the signal candle's high.
- VWAP (E 00:14:00-00:17:00, 00:21:00, 00:29:00): price tends to return to VWAP; never buy far above VWAP or sell far
  below it; the best sell is from above or near VWAP.
- Stop (E 00:26:00): the Supertrend line (his example: entry 18,395, the Supertrend line about 50 points below).
  ctpGuhCCAtE puts it at the high of the third candle back (E 00:08:30-00:09:00).
- Exit (E 00:17:00-00:20:00, 00:27:00-00:28:00): book 50% at the next opposite MACD cross; exit the rest when the
  Supertrend flips; an intraday trade is closed before the session ends.
- ctpGuhCCAtE adds: it works best in a volatile market (India VIX above about 16-20, E 00:01:00-00:02:30) and the
  15-minute MACD should agree (E 00:10:00-00:10:30). Adding size on a pullback doji or hammer (0EXVG4kPAm0 E
  00:24:00-00:27:00) is not implemented. The "70% accuracy" is his claim, not tested in the video.
- A: the NSE 5-minute stock pool (he says it works on stocks too; the desk's BankNifty has no volume for VWAP);
  Supertrend (20, 2) with RMA-smoothed ATR; MACD (12, 26, 9); the flip and the MACD cross in the same direction at most
  4 candles apart, the later one being the signal candle; for a buy the signal candle closes no more than 1 x ATR 14
  above the session VWAP (a sell: no more than 1 x ATR below it); a stop-entry one tick above the signal candle's high
  (below its low) valid for the next 2 candles; a hard stop at the Supertrend value of the signal candle (skip if it is
  more than 3 x ATR away); half off at the next open after an opposite MACD cross; the rest at the next open after a
  close beyond the Supertrend line; entries 09:30-14:30, out at 15:15; one trade per stock per day.

## PR-02 9:20 opening range: wait for the retest, 1:3 (YW5oDzVfWeU, 1 Apr 2025, 27 min, 728k views)

Read in full.

- E 00:01:00-00:02:00, 00:06:00: BankNifty on 1-minute candles; the opening range is the high and low of 09:15-09:20
  (the first five 1-minute candles).
- E 00:02:30-00:04:00, 00:13:00, 00:18:00: do not buy the breakout itself (most people do and get trapped); wait for
  price to come back and retest the broken edge; if it falls back inside the range there is no trade; enter when a
  bullish candle forms after the retest (a hammer then a bullish candle in his first example).
- E 00:04:00, 00:11:00, 00:22:00: stop at the closest swing but at least 50 BankNifty points; target at least 1:3
  (1:2 for beginners).
- E 00:23:00-00:24:00: the trade should work within about two hours; after that, exit if the move has stalled.
- E 00:07:30: also works on liquid NIFTY 50 stocks.
- A: BANKNIFTY 1-minute; range = the 09:15-09:19 candles; a breakout = a close above the range high (below the low);
  a retest = a later candle whose low comes within 0.1 x the range height of the range high while its close stays
  above it; that side is cancelled when a candle closes back inside the range; entry at the next open after the first
  green (red) candle that follows the retest; stop 0.05% below the lowest low since the breakout, widened to at least
  0.1% (his 50 points at about 51,000); target 3R; time stop 120 minutes; entries until 13:00; one trade a day.

## PR-03 9:20 gap against the trend, opposite first candle (HONlprzggHU, 20 Nov 2022, 30 min, 3.8M views; guest)

Read the rule parts in full.

- When (E 00:00:30-00:02:00, 00:28:00): the market has trended for three or four sessions and then opens with a big
  gap against that trend (300-500 BankNifty points then, at about 41,000-42,000).
- Entry (E 00:03:30-00:04:30, 00:19:00): 5-minute chart; watch the first candle (09:15-09:20); after a gap down it
  must close green (the opposite colour) or there is no trade; buy as soon as its high breaks. A downtrend and a big
  gap up is the mirror (a red first candle, sell its low).
- Stop (E 00:04:30-00:05:00): 70-80 points when the first candle is under 100 points; 100 points when it is 100-150.
- Exit (E 00:06:00, 00:09:30-00:12:00): the previous close acts as resistance and is the target; after a move of
  more than 150 points, exit if price falls back 50 points from the high.
- Rare (E 00:21:00): about six trades a year.
- A: BANKNIFTY 5-minute; trend = the previous close above (below) the close four sessions earlier; gap = the open at
  least 0.6% below (above) the previous close; the first candle closes green (red); a stop-entry at its high (low) from
  09:20, valid until 10:30; stop 0.18% (his 75 points at 41,000) when the first candle's range is under 0.25% of price,
  else 0.25%; target = the previous close; once the trade is 0.37% (150 points) in profit, exit when price gives back
  0.12% (50 points) from its best; out at 15:15; one trade a day.

## PR-05 Ghanshyam Tech's window strategy, BankNifty 1 minute (EISy2AIXZrg, 30 Nov 2022, 12 min, 834k views)

Read in full.

- Window (E 00:01:30-00:03:30): the 30 points above a round hundred (x00-x30); the first 1-minute candle's high must
  lie inside it (x31, or x99 just below, means no trade).
- Trigger (E 00:02:00-00:02:30): the high must break on the second or third candle, no later; buy at once on the
  break.
- Sell (E 00:05:00-00:05:30): the mirror, with the first candle's low in the 30 points below a round hundred
  (x70-x00).
- Stop (E 00:04:00): the second candle's low, about 80-90 points.
- Exit (E 00:06:00): ride the 1-minute momentum until it reverses; do not switch to 5 minutes.
- Frequency (E 00:01:30): about three or four set-ups a month.
- A: BANKNIFTY 1-minute; a buy when the 09:15 candle's high mod 100 is 0-30, a sell when its low mod 100 is 70-99.99
  or exactly 0; a stop-entry at the high (low) valid for the 09:16 and 09:17 candles; stop at the first candle's low
  (high), since the trigger candle's own low is not known when the order fills; exit at the next open after the first
  1-minute close below the previous candle's low (above its high); out by 10:15; one trade a day.

## PR-08 Pivot points: sell the rally to the pivot, stop beyond R1 (qZYsR4mivbQ, 11 Oct 2022, 39 min; guest)

Read the rule parts in full.

- Bias (E 00:09:30-00:11:00): at the open, is the stock above or below the day's pivot point? Below = sell on rallies
  only; above = buy on dips only.
- Entry (E 00:12:30-00:13:30): do not short blindly at the 09:30 close; wait for price to come back to the pivot and
  have a sell order there.
- Stop and targets (E 00:14:00-00:15:00, 00:16:00): stop at R1, set one step beyond it (1,429 for an R1 of 1,428);
  first target S1, then S2; trail the stop.
- E 00:18:00-00:20:00: when a stock opens far below the pivot (between S3 and S4) he does not chase it; the closer the
  entry to the pivot, the smaller the risk.
- A: the NSE 5-minute stock pool (HDFC Bank and Bandhan Bank in the video); classic pivots from the previous session
  (P = (H + L + C) / 3, R1 = 2P - L, S1 = 2P - H, R2 = P + (H - L), S2 = P - (H - L)); bias from the 09:30 close
  (below P = shorts only, above = longs only, beyond S2 or R2 = no trade); a limit at P valid until 13:30; stop 0.07%
  beyond R1 (S1); half at S1 (R1) with the stop moved to the entry, the rest at S2 (R2); out at 15:15; one trade per
  stock per day.

## PR-10 RSI divergence with a close-line trigger (2bHtkFnK0AM, 9 Mar 2024, 17 min, 1.1M views)

Read in full.

- Set-up (E 00:01:00-00:01:30, 00:03:30): the "RSI Divergence" indicator on TradingView; 5- or 15-minute charts (not
  30 or 60 minutes, which misalign with the 09:15 open).
- Trigger (E 00:04:30-00:06:00): draw a horizontal line at the close of the candle that gave the bullish divergence;
  wait for a red candle to close below it; that red candle's low must then be broken; the first green candle that
  closes above the line is the buy, at its close.
- Stop (E 00:09:30): just beyond the lowest point, with a 0.1 buffer. Target (E 00:07:00-00:08:00, 00:10:00): 1:2,
  or the swing high where the fall began.
- Bearish signals mirror it (E 00:08:30-00:09:30).
- A: NIFTY 15-minute; RSI 14; TradingView's divergence defaults (pivot lookback 5 left and 5 right, the earlier pivot
  5-60 bars back); a bullish signal = a price pivot low below the previous one with a higher RSI pivot low, known 5
  bars after the pivot; everything after that: (1) a red candle closes below the pivot candle's close, (2) a later
  candle trades below that red candle's low, (3) the first green candle closing above the line; enter at the next
  open; stop 0.05% below the lowest low since the pivot; target 2R; the set-up expires after 30 candles; entries until
  14:30, out at 15:15.

## PR-12 Gold: 4-hour Supertrend, half in at once, the rest on a 0.5% grid, 1% counter-scalps (uROK24QxqYk, 7 Apr
2026, 27 min)

Read in full. A sponsored copy-trading algo (two gold tokens, PAXG and XAUT, on a crypto exchange).

- Trend (E 00:03:30-00:04:00, 00:21:00): Supertrend (10, 3) on the 4-hour chart; always in the trend's direction,
  long or short.
- Rule 1 (E 00:05:30): enter only half the planned size at the signal.
- Rule 2 (E 00:08:30-00:09:00): the other half in $100 steps every 0.5% against the entry until the full size.
- Rule 3 (E 00:09:30-00:13:00, 00:15:00-00:17:00): every 1% in favour, sell a small amount of the second gold token
  with a 1% target, only while the long is at least twice that size; the sideways chop pays these small profits.
- The loss case (E 00:20:00): the grid fills and the trend flips. His 4-hour backtest on PAXG was profitable with a
  0.05% fee per side (E 00:20:00-00:21:00).
- A: XAUUSD (Delta's XAUTUSD) 1-hour bars built into 4-hour candles from 00:00 UTC; Supertrend (10, 3); the planned
  size = 1 x the account's equity in notional; at a flip, 50% at the next open in the new direction; five 10% limits
  at 0.5%, 1.0%, 1.5%, 2.0% and 2.5% against that entry; at every further 1% in favour of the first entry, a 10%
  opposite leg with a 1% target (opened only while the open trend legs are at least twice the open counter legs); no
  stop; every leg closes at the next open after the opposite flip, which starts the next phase. Each leg is a trade.

## PR-13 Bitcoin: the same ladder with a 10% take-profit (LH_0SxIoz8Y, 13 Apr 2026, 15 min)

- At +10% (E 00:08:00): book the whole position, then leave a fresh small position to run.
- Version 2 (E 00:10:00): half at the signal and the rest every 0.5% down to the full size by about 2.5% down.

  trend leg is opened at that price and runs to the flip; every open leg closes at the next open after the opposite
  flip; no stop.
