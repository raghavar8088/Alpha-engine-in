# Brad Goh, The Trading Geek (@TheTradingGeek): video analyses

Tags as in neerajjoshi.md: E = stated or shown; I = inferred; A = research assumption. Times HH:MM:SS. English
captions. Most of his 2024-2026 videos teach one smart-money framework (higher-timeframe break of structure, a point
of interest, a lower-timeframe liquidity sweep, a 2R-3R target); the distinct entries are kept below and the rest
are listed at the end as covered. Note: IITian Trader's and Tanisha Garg's videos with the same titles ("BEST Gold
Scalping Strategy (Beginner to PRO)", "How I Combine Liquidity Sweeps, Order Blocks and FVGs for SNIPER Entries")
are their own Hindi videos with different content (analysed in their files).

## BG-01 Gold: 1-hour structure, 15-minute sweep at the point of interest (en8RMFRqSME, 31 Jul 2026, 28 min)

- Steps (chapters 00:03:01-00:13:45): (1) mark the market shift and the latest break of structure (bias); (2) the
  point of interest (supply/demand zone, order block, FVG) the move came from; (3) the obvious liquidity (swing lows,
  equal lows, long wicks) price must take; (4) at the point of interest wait for the sweep on 15 minutes: never enter
  on the first touch; (5) target the next 15-minute swing high (scalping, about 1:3), the 1-hour high is too far.
- Entry: aggressive = right after the sweep, stop below the candle that swept (the protected low, E 00:13:00);
  conservative = wait for the lower-timeframe market shift and enter on the pullback to the demand zone that caused
  it (E 00:14:30-00:15:00). His live gold trades waited for one extra candle in the trade's direction (E 00:19:00).
- A: XAUUSD; 1-hour bias = the latest break of a confirmed 5-candle fractal (a close above the last fractal high =
  bullish); point of interest = the last bearish 1-hour candle before that break (its high-low); on 15 minutes, while
  price is inside the zone, a candle trades below a confirmed 3-candle fractal low and closes back above it; enter at
  the next open after one bullish 15-minute candle closes; stop 0.1 x ATR below the sweep low; target the nearest
  confirmed 15-minute fractal high if at least 1.5R away, else 2R; mirror for shorts; one trade per zone.

## BG-02 The one "liquidation candle" scalp, 2R (xG8jxyTp7uc, 16 Aug 2025, 25 min)

- E 00:02:00-00:04:00: a candle with a long wick and a small body; the longer the wick and the smaller the body, the
  better; it must appear in context: where price sweeps equal lows/highs or a swing.
- Timeframes (E 00:04:30): 5 minutes for the trend (or 1 minute when 5 minutes is ranging), entries on 1-5 minutes.
- Entry (E 00:07:30, 00:08:30): when price sweeps the lows and prints the liquidation candle, place a buy order
  above it; stop below its wick; target a constant 2R (for the video; normally the next point of interest).
- A: XAUUSD 5-minute; liquidation candle = wick on the sweep side at least 2 x the body and at least 60% of the range;
  it trades beyond a confirmed 3-candle fractal low (high) from the last 24 candles and closes back inside; a stop-
  entry above its high (below its low) valid 2 candles; stop 0.1 x ATR beyond the wick; target 2R.

## BG-03 1-minute scalp: 15-minute point of interest, 1-minute sweep zone retest (thbpmdMYuu0, 26 Jul 2025, 26 min;
cFzD500aQFM live)

- E 00:00:30-00:05:00: higher timeframe 15 minutes, lower 1 minute (5 minutes for less experienced traders). (1) a
  15-minute break of structure; (2) its point of interest (demand zone; refine it to the pivot candle that led to the
  break for a tighter stop, E 00:11:00-00:12:00); (3) when price taps it, on 1 minute wait for a liquidity sweep
  (equal lows taken) or a failed reaction, then for price to pull back to the most recent lower-timeframe zone that
  swept liquidity: enter there; stop below that zone; target 2R.
- A: XAUUSD; 15-minute bias and zone as BG-01's but on 15 minutes; on 1 minute a sweep of a confirmed 3-candle fractal
  low inside the zone; the sweep zone = the sweeping candle's body; a limit at its top, valid 30 one-minute candles;
  stop 0.1 x 1-minute ATR below the sweep low; target 2R.

## BG-04 Asia range sweep at the London open (oC7ssQOn8Fs, 6 Mar 2026, 36 min)

- Asia range (E 00:01:33-00:04:30): 19:00-03:00 New York time (a tighter 20:00-01:00 also works); the high and low
  are the extreme wicks.
- When not to trade (E 00:05:13-00:06:27): news; a tiny range (no liquidity, fake-outs); no range at all (a clean
  trend).
- When (E 00:06:27-00:08:00): the London open, 03:00-06:00 New York time.
- Entry (E 00:10:00-00:12:00, 00:20:00-00:24:00): price takes out the Asia high or low, rejects it (a liquidation
  candle, or a close back inside) and shows displacement the other way; aggressive = enter right after the rejection;
  conservative = wait for the failed reaction (a higher low) and enter there.
- Stop (E 00:12:00): a few pips beyond the liquidation candle's wick. Target 2R.
- His GBPUSD example (1.34830 level).
- A: GBPUSD 5-minute; skip if the Asia range is below 25% or above 90% of the 20-day average daily range; a 5-minute
  candle between 03:00 and 06:00 New York trades beyond the Asia high (low) and closes back inside; within the next 3
  candles a candle with a body at least 1.5 x the 20-candle average closes in the reversal direction; enter at the
  next open; stop 0.1 x ATR beyond the sweep extreme; target 2R; one trade a day.

## BG-05 Gold supply/demand zone with an EMA 8 cross and an engulfing (AY7fWmE1CBk, 30 Nov 2022, 15 min)

- Zones (E 00:00:30-00:02:00): a demand zone is where an aggressive rally began (several green candles in a row); a
  supply zone where an aggressive drop began; trade the first retest.
- Entry (E 00:04:00-00:05:30): at the zone (1-hour chart), on 15 minutes wait for two confirmations: the 8 (or 9) EMA
  cross (price crossing back through it) and a candlestick pattern (a big engulfing candle in the trade's direction).
- Stop above the supply zone or above the last lower high; target the next zone (E 00:05:30).
- A: XAUUSD; a zone = the base candle before 3+ same-colour 1-hour candles covering at least 2 x ATR 14; first retest
  only; on 15 minutes a close back across EMA 8 that is also an engulfing candle; enter at the next open; stop 0.1 x
  ATR beyond the zone; target 2R; zones expire after 10 days.

## BG-07 Silver bullet, his "golden bullet" version (rU0vQUTNXVE, 17 Jul 2026, 30 min)

- Five steps (E 00:01:45-00:08:00): price sweeps liquidity (above a swing high / below a swing low); a clean
  displacement the other way; price pulls back into an entry model (FVG, order block, demand zone, flip zone); enter
  there with the stop beyond that point of interest; target the opposing liquidity (the next low or high).
- Timing (E 00:08:00): the time windows matter (the ICT silver-bullet hours); indices and forex examples.
- Overlap: CP-28 (crypto playlist) is a silver-bullet strategy on bitcoin; BG-07 is Brad Goh's on the Nasdaq.
- A: NAS100 5-minute, 10:00-11:00 New York; a sweep of a confirmed 3-candle fractal from the session; displacement =
  a candle with body at least 1.5 x average leaving an FVG; a limit at the FVG's near edge valid until 11:30; stop
  beyond the sweep extreme plus 0.1 x ATR; target the nearest opposing fractal if at least 2R away, else 2R.

## BG-08 Candle range theory on 15 minutes (q02_LyIXZlk, 3 Apr 2026, 24 min)

- E 00:02:09-00:10:00: the candle range = a 15-minute candle's high and low; good places: pullbacks in the higher-
  timeframe trend, equal highs/lows, prior session highs/lows. Wait for price to sweep the range's high or low, then
  drop to 5 minutes.
- Entries (E 00:11:10-00:16:50): aggressive = after the sweep on the liquidation candle; conservative = after the 5-
  minute market shift (failed reaction). Stop a few pips beyond the liquidation candle (or the range low); target the
  opposite end of the range.
- A: XAUUSD; a range candle = a 15-minute candle with a range at least 1.2 x ATR 14; within the next 4 fifteen-minute
  candles, a 5-minute candle trades beyond its low (high) and closes back inside; enter at the next 5-minute open;
  stop 0.1 x 5-minute ATR beyond the sweep extreme; target the range's other end if at least 1.5R away (else skip).

## BG-09 Break and retest with a confirmation candle, 1:3 (2oLhwj1E8aM, 18 Sep 2022, 15 min)

- E 00:00:30-00:02:30: only in a clear trend; price breaks the structural high (the last high before a pullback);
  do not buy the break: wait for the retest of that level and for the continuation.
- Entry (E 00:07:42-00:12:00): at the retest look for a doji followed by a big candle in the trend's direction,
  multiple wick rejections, or an engulfing; then enter; stop beyond the key level; target 3 x the stop.
- A: EURUSD 15-minute; trend = EMA 50 rising and price above it (mirror for shorts); structural high = a confirmed
  5-candle fractal high broken by a close; the retest = a low within 0.25 x ATR of that level within 20 candles; the
  confirmation = a bullish engulfing or a candle with a lower wick at least 2 x its body closing above the level;
  enter at the next open; stop 0.25 x ATR below the level; target 3R.

## BG-10 Consolidation breakout on a momentum candle (DKohGRr18ZA, 19 Aug 2022, 8 min)

- E 00:01:30-00:03:30: skip pattern names; draw the consolidation's key levels; 80% of breakouts fail, so require a
  momentum candle (a big candle, or several medium ones) on the break; enter right away (most breakouts never come
  back for a retest).
- Exit (E 00:04:00): half at 1:3, stop to breakeven, the rest until the moving-average crossover (his EMA 8/14).
- A: EURUSD 15-minute; a consolidation = the last 16 candles inside a band no taller than 2 x ATR 14; a close beyond
  it by a candle whose body is at least 1.8 x the 20-candle average; enter at the next open; stop at the momentum
  candle's other end; half off at 3R with the stop to breakeven; the rest exits at the next open after EMA 8 crosses
  back through EMA 14.
