## SB-01 9/20 EMA: ride the trend from the first pullback, exit on a close beyond EMA 20, stocks (r96JUvEANlE, 17 Jun
2024, 27 min, 514k views; CXhidQTuhEU, 12 Jun 2026; RiB6hiAqscQ, 11 Dec 2024)

Read the rule parts of r96JUvEANlE in full.

- Set-up (E 00:04:00, 00:10:00-00:11:00, 00:21:00): a range or level breaks with rising volume; after the breakout,
  the first pullback takes support near EMA 9 (EMA 20 below it); enter there.
- Stop (E 00:11:00): below the swing low, or out on a strong candle closing below EMA 20 (a weak rejection candle
  below it may be given one more candle, E 00:11:00-00:11:30).
- Exit (E 00:12:00, 00:21:00-00:23:00): hold while price stays above EMA 20; exit when a candle closes below EMA 20.
  Add near EMA 9 while the trend holds (E 00:21:00). Shorts mirror it.
- Works on 5 minutes intraday and on daily charts for swings (E 00:17:00, 00:20:30); his stock example gained about
  10% in two days (E 00:21:30).
- A: the NSE 5-minute stock pool; EMA 9 above EMA 20; a breakout = a close above the highest high of the previous 12
  candles on volume at least 1.5 x its 20-candle average; within the next 10 candles, the first candle whose low
  reaches EMA 9 and that closes above EMA 9 (and above EMA 20); enter at the next open; stop 0.1 x ATR 14 below that
  candle's low; exit at the next open after a close below EMA 20; out at 15:15; entries 09:30-14:30; one trade per
  stock per day; shorts mirror it.

## SB-02 9/20 EMA pullback on 15 minutes, crypto (ppuEOzpEYlg, 19 Nov 2025, 15 min; lp57qG4Evj0, 15 May 2025;
GZBl62jO8SY, 5 Aug 2025; EmGHT8ASB0M, 15 Jun 2026)

Read ppuEOzpEYlg in full and the others in digest.

- Direction (E 00:03:00-00:04:00): below both EMAs = sells only; above both = buys only; when price crosses the EMAs
  back and forth the market is in a range: no trades.
- Entry (E 00:04:30-00:05:30): a pullback that fails to close back above EMA 20 (rejected), a negative candle, and
  the entry when price breaks the low of that big candle.
- Stop and target (E 00:06:30-00:07:30): stop at the swing high; target a logical level (a round number, the next
  support); he made about 1:2.5. The 1-hour chart is more accurate for crypto swings (E 00:08:00, lp57qG4Evj0
  00:09:30).
- A: BTCUSD 15-minute; a short set-up = EMA 9 below EMA 20 and the last two closes below both; a pullback candle whose
  high reaches EMA 9 but closes below EMA 20 and is red; a stop-entry one tick below its low, valid for 3 candles; stop
  0.1 x ATR above the highest high of the last 5 candles; target 2.5R; skip if price crossed EMA 20 more than twice in
  the last 12 candles (a range); longs mirror it.

## SB-03 9 EMA rejection scalp, NIFTY 5 minutes (AO5tS1hqMao, 17 Apr 2024, 27 min, 647k views; Gc-xw57Zrg8, 3 Jun
2026)

Read the rule parts of both in full.

- Direction (Gc-xw57Zrg8 E 00:04:00-00:05:00; AO5tS1hqMao E 00:08:30-00:09:30): price above EMA 9 = calls only; below
  = puts only (judged on the spot chart).
- Entry (AO5tS1hqMao E 00:09:30-00:11:30, 00:13:00): a candle that tests EMA 9 and rejects it in the trend's direction
  (a strong rejection or a strong trend candle); enter on it; stop at that candle's high (for a short).
- Exit (Gc-xw57Zrg8 E 00:06:00, AO5tS1hqMao E 00:10:00): out as soon as a candle closes on the other side of EMA 9;
  hold at most two or three 5-minute candles (a time stop); at least 1:2 (Gc-xw57Zrg8 E 00:05:00).
- Mark 15-minute support and resistance first (E 00:07:30-00:09:30): a cross-check, not a mechanical rule. Avoid
  ranges where price whips around EMA 9.
- A: NIFTY 5-minute; a short = the last two closes below EMA 9 and a red candle whose high reaches EMA 9 while it closes
  below it, with an upper wick at least 40% of its range; enter at the next open; stop 0.02% above its high; target 2R;
  exit at the next open after a close above EMA 9; time stop 3 candles; skip if the closes crossed EMA 9 three or more
  times in the last 10 candles; entries 09:30-14:45; longs mirror it.

## SB-04 200 EMA retest with a confirmation candle, 15 minutes (vmI2dxCXPOw, 18 Jun 2025, 26 min; Zf7feCjYLdo, 3 Dec
2025)

Read the rule parts of both in full.

- Trend (vmI2dxCXPOw E 00:07:00, 00:10:00): above EMA 200 = longs only, below = shorts only.
- Entry (E 00:10:00-00:13:00): wait for price to come back and retest EMA 200; do not enter on the touch; wait for a
  confirmation candle (a rejection, then a candle in the trend's direction) and enter on it.
- Stop and target (E 00:12:30, 00:14:00): below the last swing with a small buffer; target 1:2 (1:1-1:3).
- Timeframe (Zf7feCjYLdo E 00:08:00-00:12:30): 5 minutes is noisy on bitcoin; 15 minutes is his best for crypto
  intraday (two or three trades a day); 30 minutes gives fewer, longer trades.
- A: BTCUSD 15-minute; long when the last 10 closes are above EMA 200 and a candle's low comes within 0.1 x ATR 14 of
  EMA 200 (or below it) while it closes above it; the confirmation = the next candle closes green above that candle's
  high; enter at the next open; stop 0.1 x ATR below the lowest low of the last 5 candles; target 2R; shorts mirror it.

## SB-05 The first 5-minute candle breakout, one trade a day, NIFTY (Jvg9c7Ba36M, 9 Jun 2024, 30 min)

Read the rule parts in full.

- E 00:03:00-00:04:00: the opening range is the first 5-minute candle (09:15-09:20); one set-up and one trade a day.
- Opening (E 00:04:30-00:05:30): avoid big gap-up or gap-down days (100-250 NIFTY points); the set-up works best when
  the market opens near the previous close (E 00:09:00).
- Conditions (E 00:06:00-00:09:00, 00:15:00): a trending market, or a market in a range opening near the range's edge.
- Breakout (E 00:09:30-00:10:00): the breakout candle must be healthy and strong; a doji or weak candle through the
  range is not a set-up.
- Risk (E 00:11:30-00:12:00, 00:13:00): stop at the other side of the first candle; targets of 1:3 to 1:5; book half
  and hold the rest until about 14:00.
- A: NIFTY 5-minute; skip when the open is 0.4% or more from the previous close; the trade's direction must agree with
  the 5-session trend (the previous close above the close 5 sessions earlier for a long); the breakout = a later candle
  closing beyond the first candle's high (low) with a body at least 60% of its range; enter at the next open; stop at
  the first candle's other end; half at 3R with the stop moved to the entry; the rest exits at 14:00; entries until
  12:00; one trade a day.

## SB-06 Liquidity sweep at a tested level, then a pause-candle or rejection trigger, gold (Tj_eVkcuHtM, 13 Sep 2026,
37 min; _2dwmtv1u90, 22 Sep 2026; s8l4H2BeveI, 27 Jun 2026; _oc_SpFo12Q, 9 Oct 2026)

Read Tj_eVkcuHtM's rule parts in full, the others in digest.

- Levels (Tj_eVkcuHtM E 00:08:30-00:09:30): support and resistance from the swing structure; the level must have been
  hit once before; the second visit sweeps it (E 00:18:00).
- Sweep and confirmation (E 00:16:00-00:18:00): price breaks the level with a breakout candle; then a pause candle; the
  next candle breaks the pause candle's high (for a long at support) = entry; or a rejection candle at the sweep
  (hammer, dragonfly doji).
- Stop and target (E 00:19:00-00:21:00): stop beyond the sweep's extreme (the invalidation); target the next
  liquidity point (the opposite level); at least 1:3.
- Bias (E 00:23:00-00:25:00): trade sweeps in the higher timeframe's direction (support sweeps in an uptrend); ignore
  counter-trend sweeps unless at a major higher-timeframe level. Gold examples on 1-hour levels with 15-minute entries
  gave 1:3-1:5 (_2dwmtv1u90 E 00:09:00-00:11:30).
- A: XAUUSD; levels = confirmed 5-candle fractal lows (highs) on 1-hour candles from the last 5 days; a 15-minute
  candle trades below the level; within the next 4 candles either (a) a hammer (lower wick at least 2 x the body)
  closes back above the level, entry at the next open, or (b) after a red candle that closed below the level, a pause
  candle (body at most half of that candle's body), and a stop-entry one tick above the pause candle's high, valid 2
  candles; stop 0.1 x ATR 14 below the lowest low since the sweep; target the nearest 1-hour fractal high above if at
  least 3R away, else 3R; only with the 1-hour EMA 50 rising (falling for shorts); one trade per level; shorts mirror
  it.

## SB-07 Pre-breakout: buy under a tested resistance while the lows rise (xTyycYmFb_k, 12 Dec 2025, 12 min;
-qnLeSGKBcM, 11 Sep 2025, 19 min)

Read both in full.

- Set-up (-qnLeSGKBcM E 00:05:30-00:07:30): a resistance tested once or twice, an uptrend, a strong support below,
  and the lows shifting up so price does not return to that support; price holds near the breakout zone.
- Entry (xTyycYmFb_k E 00:03:00-00:04:00, -qnLeSGKBcM E 00:09:30): orders placed in the zone before the breakout
  (15-minute structure, 30-minute entry in his ether trade); stop below the last swing low; shift the stop once the
  breakout comes; target the next swing high or zone.
- A: BTCUSD 30-minute; resistance = at least two confirmed 5-candle fractal highs within 0.25% of each other in the
  last 80 candles; the last two confirmed fractal lows after the first of those highs are rising; price above EMA 50;
  entry at the next open after a close within 0.4% below the resistance (a close above it first cancels the set-up);
  stop 0.1 x ATR 14 below the latest higher low; target = resistance + (resistance - latest higher low); after a close
  above the resistance the stop moves to the entry; time stop 48 candles; long only.
