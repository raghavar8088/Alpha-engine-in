## Contradictions noted

- **R:R:** the strategy videos require 1:2 (1:1.5 for beginners), but live calls are taken at 1:1.1–1:1.43 (nPVISNpXmtQ).
- **"No breakout entries":** stated in the ICT class (37uPX6Li-xE). Live streams still call level breakouts (hxzRM3Fb41I, dWVIXC-j7Qo).
- **Discretion:** live trades stack confluences (order blocks on two timeframes, the golden zone, the 200 EMA) without a stated minimum. Rules that need "enough confluence" cannot be tested as stated.
