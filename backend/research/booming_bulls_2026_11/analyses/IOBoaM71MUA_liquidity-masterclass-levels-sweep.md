## Level types, in his order of strength

| # | Level | Rule | Type | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Previous-day high/low (PDH/PDL) | Strongest. Drawn on **wicks**. Previous-day open and close matter for exchange markets (Nifty, Nasdaq) but not for 24-hour gold/silver | E | 05:00–05:30, 12:30, 13:30 |
| L2 | Current-day high/low | Only after the day's range is at least daily ATR minus 20–30% (gold: ATR ≈ $100, use $70–80). In a smaller range, don't trade the sweep (a breakout is possible instead) | E | 14:00–16:30, 20:00 |
| L3 | Value-area high/low (volume profile, not POC) | His favourite area; shown in detail in rFrFhpwJJOc | E | 06:00 |
| L4 | Higher-timeframe order block | 15-minute (for a 5-minute trader) or 1-hour; only the top-most and bottom-most blocks of a swing; never 5-minute order blocks | E | 06:30, 20:30–21:30, 24:00 |
| L5 | Swing POC | For swing trades; deferred to a later class | E (not taught) | 08:00, 24:30 |

## Entry and exit

| # | Rule | Type | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Trade only after a **sweep** (price trades through the level); never on a touch | E | 13:30–14:00 "hameshaa sveep … nevar evar treding on d tach" |
| T2 | **Confirmation:** a candle closes back across the level. No close back means no entry, even if a retest appears | E | 12:00, 17:00 "kyaa main mere leval ke oopar kloj diyaa? naheen" |
| T3 | **Stop:** beyond the sweep extreme, not tight. Example: low near 4304 → stop at least $3 lower, under the 4300 round number | E | 18:30–19:00 |
| T4 | **Target:** the next liquidity level (e.g. the next day's high), risk:reward about 1:2 | E / I | 12:00, 22:00 "risk rivord too kaa chaahie thaa" |
| T5 | If an order-block stop is too wide (e.g. $21), cut size with a risk calculator, or drop to 5-minute and enter on a fair-value-gap retest with a ~$2 stop | E | 22:00–23:00 |
| T6 | Examples on a 15-minute chart: wider stop, wider target | E | 19:00 |
| T7 | Night hours and event candles: he doesn't trade them (gold at midnight is "not tradable") | E (time window unstated) | 11:00, 12:30 |
