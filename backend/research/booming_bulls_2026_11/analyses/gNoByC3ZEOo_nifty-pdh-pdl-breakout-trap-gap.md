## Discretionary or unclear

- "Too wide" a stop, a "big" candle and a "good" candle are never quantified beyond the examples (₹80 big candle; stops 22–48 points used).
- Stop placement is chosen while the outcome is already visible: "the stop will hit here" (18:30, 23:15), "1:2 won't happen" (27:15).
- The wick rule is waived on 25 Sep (30:45) and applied on 29 Sep (32:30).
- On 5 Sep the captions say "buy" but the stop goes "a little above" the 50% level, which fits a short (needs the chart).
