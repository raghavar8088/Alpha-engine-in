## Rules

| # | Rule | Type | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15-minute chart, session breaks on | E | 02:30, 06:00 |
| 2 | At **10:00** (his clock, India time), mark the day's high and low so far on candle **bodies** | E | 01:00–01:30, 03:00 |

| 4 | Entries only from the **11:45** candle onward | E | 01:30 |

| 6 | **Trap:** a candle closes outside the range, then a candle closes back inside → sell at a high-side trap, buy at a low-side trap | E | 01:30–02:00, 09:30 |
| 7 | Skip a bad entry candle (adverse wick); wait for a good one | E | 11:00, 14:00 |
| 8 | **Stop:** just beyond the trap candle's wick, with some space (one or two candles). If it is very large, put it at about 50–60% of the range | E | 03:30–04:00, 04:30, 13:00 |
| 9 | **Target:** fixed 1:2. No breakeven or trailing in the base rules | E | 02:00 |
| 10 | One entry a day; after a stop, no more trades that day | E | 05:30–06:00 |
