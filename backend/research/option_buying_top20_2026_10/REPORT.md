## How it was tested

- The library: every option-buying strategy in the app (strategy-service `options_buying`, the Pre-Live desk's library): 167 strategy and timeframe pairs (5-minute, 15-minute, 1-hour), on NIFTY 50.
- The live desk's rules: a fresh instance every session warmed on 400 bars; a signal at each bar close unless a position is open; at most 6 trades a day; BUY buys the at-the-money call, SELL the at-the-money put, of the nearest weekly expiry; one lot of 65; the category's premium stop and target (scalp 25% / 50%, intraday 30% / 60%, breakout 35% / 100%), else out at 15:15.
- Prices on 5–23 Oct 2026: the REAL quotes the app's recorder kept every minute, bought at the ask and sold at the bid. On 24 Sep – 3 Oct (no recorder yet): the premium model validated in the NIFTY options study (previous day's real NSE prices, India VIX, intraday variance clock), with half the spread and a tick on each fill. The persistence check uses the model for every month.
- Charges: Angel One's option rate card at today's rates on every trade.
- Real recorded quotes are one snapshot a minute, so a stop or target is checked once a minute, not on every tick.
