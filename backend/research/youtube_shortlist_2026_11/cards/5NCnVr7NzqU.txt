summary: Krown studies every vertical bitcoin run since 2011 (1,600 backtested versions, declustered) to test the common plan of waiting for a 2-15% dip before buying. Shallow dips fill about half the time, saving a few percent; when they never fill, the median chase is about 23.5%. Dips of 10% or more fill only 17-28% of the time, and most fills come within about 48 hours, so waiting for the dip is really a 48-hour plan. He adds that bitcoin's weekly candles rarely stay disconnected from the weekly 5 EMA for more than two closes without a retest, and gives his own levels and cycle targets.
