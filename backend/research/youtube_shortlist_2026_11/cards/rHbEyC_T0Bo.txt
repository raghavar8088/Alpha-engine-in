summary: Lark Davis shows three daily crypto breakout trades. One: a downtrend line drawn along the lower peaks breaks (often after a first fakeout), then retests. Two: an all-time-high breakout on a daily candle close, with the stop just under the old high. Three: a resistance breakout to the next resistance level, where broken resistance turns into support. He prefers daily and weekly charts and expects some stops on fakeouts. The examples are bitcoin, Sui and Dogecoin.
