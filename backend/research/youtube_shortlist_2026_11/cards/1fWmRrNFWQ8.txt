summary: A Pepperstone webinar (FX Evolution) on scalping with the MACD and the stochastic. First recognise the trend on the 4-hour (ideally the daily too): price clearly above or below the 200 moving average, with higher highs and lows (or lower). Then, on the 15-minute, take the MACD signal-line cross out of the histogram in the trend's direction after a pullback toward the 200 (or the 20/50 EMAs). Stops go beyond the recent highs or lows, sized from the last candle's range. Targets are higher-timeframe support or resistance, about 3:1 in the example. The stochastic leaving overbought or oversold is an alternative trigger, and a points checklist (at least 75) gates trades. The London open is the best session, Asia the worst.
instruments: Forex pairs (NZD/USD, GBP/USD)
timeframes: 4h trend, 15m entries
style: scalping
