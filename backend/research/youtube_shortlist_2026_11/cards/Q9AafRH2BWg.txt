summary: VasilyTrader's gold gap strategy. Gold's gaps are mostly weekend opening gaps, priced from news while the market is closed, and most get filled back to the gap opening level (the last close before the weekend). On the 1-hour or 4-hour chart, after a gap down: wait for a test of a demand zone, then a consolidation under a minor resistance. Buy the candle that closes above that resistance, or set a limit on its retest. The stop goes below the consolidation's low and the target a couple of pips below the gap opening level. Gap ups mirror this below a minor support. He needs at least 1.1R and shows a live trade that filled overnight.
