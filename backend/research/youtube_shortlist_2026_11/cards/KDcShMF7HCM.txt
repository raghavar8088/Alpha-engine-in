summary: Crypto Crew University (Steve) gives his own four Ichimoku rules on the two-week bitcoin chart, to call market-cycle tops. One: the lagging (yellow) line turning down marks that the top is in. Two: the red line crossing below the blue confirms it, about 154 days after the top in past cycles. Three: the yellow line crossing down through the blue near the top. Four: a flat shelf on the blue line marks a top. The rules are about cycle tops, not trades.
