summary: MambaFx explains how he scalps forex mainly through trade management. Take a partial at 1:1, then trail the stop behind price: at 1:2 the stop moves to 1:1, and so on, so a 1:1 trade can run to 1:5 or more and can no longer lose. His five-minute entries come from support and resistance in the higher timeframe's direction (for example, a break above the moving averages with the stop just below) or from an 'M' double top targeting the pattern's low.
