summary: Fefe on Crypto Banter breaks down his crypto scalping. Read market structure on every timeframe (higher highs and lows, lower highs and lows) and trade only with the hourly trend: in an hourly downtrend only shorts. When price retraces into a rejection area, drop to the 5-minute (or 15-minute) and enter on a market structure shift in the trend's direction. The stop goes at the high of the rejection zone (about half a percent, run at 10-20x leverage). Take 1:1 to 1:2 and close half early. Liquidity (equal highs and lows, trend lines, sessions) shows where moves are heading.
