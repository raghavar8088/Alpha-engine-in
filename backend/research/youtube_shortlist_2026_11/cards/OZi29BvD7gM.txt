summary: Chart Champions explains the framework behind a day-trading strategy it says wins over 80% of the time, traded on ES futures and crypto. Read naked price action first: previous supply and demand zones and their flips. Then market structure: a low, a high and a higher low; in a range, trade only the extremes. Every level on the chart must carry a statistic from at least 200 backtested observations, and confluences such as a naked weekly POC, the anchored VWAP or the 0.75 Fibonacci of the range are added. Never enter at the level itself: wait for a reaction or confirmation candle formation, which is taught in their course. ES examples include a $90,000 trade and a $7,000 winner.
