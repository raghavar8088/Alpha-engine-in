summary: The Trading Channel (Steven Hart) explains his 4-hour swing strategy, built on CEST: conditions, entries, stops, targets. On the daily chart, an uptrend starts only when the highest high made above the 50 EMA is broken (a 'one-two-three' move); the broken high becomes the zone for buys. Downtrends mirror this with lows below the 50 EMA, and everything in between is consolidation. When price pulls back to the zone, he looks on the 4-hour (or 1-hour) chart for a candlestick combination: a shooting star, hammer or engulfing candle followed by a candle in the trade's direction. He enters at the close of that second candle, with the stop beyond the pattern or zone (an ATR, or about 20 pips on the 4-hour) and a 2:1 target. He uses it on forex, crypto and stocks.
