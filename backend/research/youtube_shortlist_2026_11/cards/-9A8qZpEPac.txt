summary: TraderNick backtests the technical part of his gold swing approach on daily XAUUSD in Forex Tester Online. A 20-period moving average and a 50-period simple moving average set the trend. In an uptrend (the averages aligned) he buys pullbacks that reach an oversold reading on a short-period RSI, with the stop below the market-structure support to the left, risking 1% a trade and taking no profit target. The stop is trailed below each new market-structure low or breakout candle, and shorts mirror this in a downtrend at an overbought reading. He also adds a second 1% position after a new high. Twelve trades over about two years returned 15.37%: small losses, small wins and the occasional large win.
