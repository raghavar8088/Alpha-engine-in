summary: VasilyTrader's simple gold trend strategy uses two default indicators on the 4-hour chart. Buy when the SMA 9 crosses above the EMA 20, confirmed only if the next 4-hour candle closes with the SMA still above; enter right after that close. The stop goes 2 ATR (of the last 4-hour candle) below the entry. There is no fixed target: as the EMA 20 rises, the stop trails at the EMA minus 1 ATR. His example made more than 3,000 pips before the trailing stop closed it. He shows bullish trades only.
