summary: TradingLab's way to avoid false breakouts. Mark a consolidation with several rejections at support and resistance; only trade a break confirmed by a momentum candle: one big body mostly beyond the level, or three candles in the breakout's direction. Enter at that candle's close, stop just beyond the broken level, take half at 1.5R and move the stop to that level, and exit the rest when the Chandelier Exit (ATR multiplier 2) changes colour.
