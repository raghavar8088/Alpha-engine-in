summary: Rayner Teo shares a Bollinger Band trend-following system for US stocks (credited to other traders), said to have made 3,227% from 1995 to 2021. Trade only while the Russell 1000 is above its 300-day moving average. Buy when a Russell 1000 stock closes above its upper Bollinger Band (200 periods, 4 standard deviations), and sell at the next open after a close below the lower band (200 periods, 0.5 standard deviations). Each position gets 10% of capital, at most ten at once, with setups ranked by their 300-day rate of change. Results: 13.86% a year, a 56.31% win rate, an average win of 32.6% against an average loss of 10%, and a 38% maximum drawdown (buy and hold about 55%).
