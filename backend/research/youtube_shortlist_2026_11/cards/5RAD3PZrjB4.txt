summary: Chart Champions' 2020 range strategy for crypto and forex rests on three questions: is support or resistance clear, is there confluence, and is price action in your favour? Draw a 4-hour (or daily) level that held several times and check that their Fibonacci 'CC' zone lines up with it. On the lower timeframe (15 or 5 minutes, or 1-4 hours), short a shooting star or bearish engulfing candle at resistance, or buy a hammer or bullish engulfing at support. No pattern means no trade. The stop goes beyond the trigger candle, and the trade always closes at the range's other side, the Fibonacci's zero point. Examples on bitcoin and ether show 1:20 and 1:25.
