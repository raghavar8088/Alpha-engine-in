summary: Tradeciety's five-minute trend-continuation strategy for forex, indices, crypto, commodities and gold. An EMA 100 sets the side: above it only longs, below it only shorts. Bollinger Bands use one standard deviation. After a strong trend wave, look for a correction that closes outside the opposite band but cannot stay there: back inside within two or three candles. Then enter on the break into a new high (new low for shorts), or a break and retest. The stop goes behind the break candle, or more conservatively beyond the opposite band. The target is a fixed 3:1 or 4:1, or a trail along the outer band until a close back inside it. His examples show 4.5R, 2.8R and 6.5R.
