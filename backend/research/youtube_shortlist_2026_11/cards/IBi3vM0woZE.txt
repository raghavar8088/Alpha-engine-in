summary: Benjamin Cowen explains Heikin-Ashi candles (close = the average of open, high, low and close; open = the midpoint of the previous candle) and uses weekly to two-month Heikin-Ashi charts to read bitcoin's momentum shifts. Uptrends show large bodies, long upper shadows and no lower shadows, and downtrends the reverse. Sideways stretches that feel like downtrends show up clearly. The candles lag, but they help holders stay with a trend. He counts the run of green two-month candles in past cycles and ties this to his cycle view; no trade rules are given.
