## TR-02 v1: Daily EMA 9 side, a 5-minute supply or demand box, one of his three entry candles, the candle's extreme as the stop, 1:2, three trades a day (Nifty, Bank Nifty)

Source: https://youtu.be/TU88i946Bgw (How To Start Trading? | Beginner To Advance EP-1| The Trade Room || Mayank Raj, 2025-01-03, 60 min). Desk markets: Nifty 50 (index, Angel One minutes), Bank Nifty (index, Angel One minutes); 1D, 5m.

His beginner strategy. Before the day, look at yesterday's daily candle against the 9 EMA: closed above it means only buys today, below it only sells. On the 5-minute chart mark where two to four candles could not break out of a range and extend that as a box: a demand box to buy from, a supply box to sell from. When the price comes back to the box, take one of his three entry candles (a pin bar, a big bar, or a big candle with its longer wick on the losing side), stop at that candle's low (high for a sell), target at least 1:2, and no more than three trades a day.

## TR-03 v1: Momentum candle through the EMA 9 and 15: a limit at its 50%, the stop beyond the candle, 1:2 (Delta gold and bitcoin, 15 minutes)
