# NSE Stock Ratings: forward-return estimates, validation report

Generated by `validate.py` from 1,259 NSE sessions (2021-10-01 to 2026-11-09), 3,379 symbols, closes adjusted for 524 bonuses, splits and consolidations, with 493 breaks (unadjustable corporate actions and one-day moves outside every NSE price band) excluded from every return.

Training (fitting) window: 2022-11-01 to 2024-10-31. Hold-out (judging) window: 2024-11-01 to 2026-11-09. Every rule below was written into `validate.py` before its result was seen, and nothing was refitted on the hold-out.

## What the estimate is

For horizon h, the log return is modelled as mu + z * sigma. Sigma is the stock's own daily volatility, a blend of a fast EWMA and its one-year level with the blend weight chosen per horizon, times sqrt(h). Mu is India's long-run market drift (ln 1.11 a year). z comes from the empirical quantiles of standardised outcomes, pooled across stocks and re-centred on 0 so the training window's own drift is not carried forward. Bear and bull are the 10th and 90th percentiles, the base case is the median, and the downside is the 5th percentile.

The hold-out was a falling market for most NSE stocks: the median 3-month outcome was -2.5% against a base case of +2.6%. The 80% ranges held a little under target, and the downside was breached more often than the 5% it claims, because the training window (a broad rally) had thinner downside tails. So, by the rule written before the run, every estimate for a horizon that missed is shown at **low confidence**, with the breach rate quoted. The 1-day horizon passed.

## What ships

`app/data/nse_forecast_calibration.json` holds the chosen model refitted on ALL outcomes (Nov 2022 to Nov 2026), so its tails include the 2024-26 decline as well as the 2022-24 rally. Confidence on the page comes from the hold-out results above, by liquidity and volatility bucket, and is never better than the horizon's own verdict. A stock gets no estimate ("Insufficient data") with fewer than 120 daily returns in a year, no trade on more than 25% of the last 63 sessions, no trade in the last 3 sessions, a BZ-series listing, or an unadjustable corporate action in the last 3 months.

These are estimates of a range, from history. They are not predictions, targets or advice.
